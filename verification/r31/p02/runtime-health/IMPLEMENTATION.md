## Actual production integration

- `RuntimeCoordinatorR26.captureHealthSnapshotR31` uses one SQL snapshot of task, typed progress, control and boot/monotonic lease with a canonical confirmed source watermark. The query returns at most 129 rows, materializes at most 128 tasks and caps text columns before materialization. Overflow/missing source/current ABI/work unit/pending becomes explicit UNKNOWN. `pending` counts tasks with known pending work, never an addition of ISSUE/PHASE/POSITION_SAMPLE units. Partial known pending is separate; any unknown makes the aggregate null.
- Scheduler QUARANTINED/NEEDS_FIX, actual drain and unresolved commit outrank otherwise fresh artifacts. A known eligible task pending beyond five canonical periods becomes STALLED independently of other tasks. Dependency/resource waits are separate. Old zero pending is not copied forward as proof; live training pending derives from committed input, and the replay dataset retains its real 360-new-row refresh threshold. Future artifact clocks are rejected.
- `healthSnapshotR31()` only reads the immutable cache. `reconcileResearchHealthR31(reason)` coalesces one asynchronous control task through `BoundedResearchExecutorR31` with a 512-byte descriptor and explicit `128*(4096+1400*4)` byte query/map estimate. This is declared admission, not measured peak heap or SQLite native memory. The root service and owner loop request control every five seconds. `activeHealthObservationR31()` does no database read, measurement or recovery; absent owner in a diagnostics PID is UNKNOWN with `MAIN_RUNTIME_OWNER_NOT_IN_THIS_PID` and PER_PID scope.
- Recovery wakes existing tasks, releases at most one eligible quarantined half-open probe through the existing rate limiter, and after two unchanged per-task artifact observations revokes the exact owner/fence/artifact generation. R27 dispatch/prepared/helper groups and R26 named continuations must actually terminate before READY. A missing R27 pool never falls through to R26 worker control. A resumed unchanged durable chunk is not repeatedly restarted; pressure pauses do not trigger revoke churn. Control requests/observed runs/revocations/pending actual drains are reported separately; none means RECOVERED until a new durable artifact exists. No new owner/pool is constructed in this controller.
- Exact-name `CooperativeContinuationR26.requestTaskCancellationR31/isTaskTerminatedR31` inspect actual threads. Task/legacy wrappers discard a cancelled terminated context even with the same owner, before reloading durable state. Computation and replay arithmetic were not changed. R27 completed cancellation receipts now require the retired owner and `fence+1`, so a late drain cannot mark a newer owner READY.
- `resources()` consumes `MemoryBudgetR31.sample`, real ActivityManager low-memory observation and thermal observations. Soft pressure reduces R27 concurrency and quantum; emergency pauses with a reason and resume condition. Registered feature caches trim under pressure, at most once per 30 seconds; no forced GC. Prepared R27 context has a 4096-byte descriptor lease. Helpers reserve a 512-byte descriptor plus `5000*10*(3*8+4*4)=2,000,000` estimated replay bytes, retaining ownership until actual body finally. The existing global four-continuation/two-helper controller remains authoritative; the extra bounded executor only runs health control, not model computations.
- Actual `MemoryBudgetR31.CapacityWait` (`RESOURCE_WAIT:MEMORY_...`) and bounded queue refusal are resource/dependency waits, not task faults; cancellation remains cancellation. Control fatal errors immediately publish UNKNOWN and the original error is retained in the executor Future, rather than leaving a healthy cache. The cache includes the actual fatal reason. Stop and probe registration share the owner signal lock; no late health read may enter after the owner starts releasing its store.
- Schema startup calls the root's journaled `migrateR31Feedback`; feedback dispatch invokes `RuntimeSchemaR26.reconcileFeedbackR31` before the existing feedback consumer and explicitly labels bridge counts `DISPATCH_NOT_MODEL_TRAINING`. The writer owns the actual feedback DDL/protocol. No runtime DDL or mainline-cache deletion was introduced here.
