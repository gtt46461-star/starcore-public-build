Success publishes the three corresponding values together and closes the old owned cache after its completed deep fork. Source audit finds only the synchronous r27-live freeze consumer of this method; this is not a general permission to close caches used by unrelated tasks. A stale concurrent change rejects the private preparation rather than overwriting a newer cache.
